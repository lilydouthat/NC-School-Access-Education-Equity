# North Carolina School Access & Education Equity

Project Overview

Educational opportunity is influenced by more than school performance alone. Geography, transportation, demographics, school choice policies, and access to alternative learning models all play a role in shaping student outcomes.

This project explores educational access and equity across the North Carolina region through geographic information systems (GIS), public education data, and policy research. The goal is to identify patterns in educational opportunity and better understand how location and access influence the educational landscape.

As an education data professional, I am interested in how organizations use information to support students, families, and communities. This project combines research, spatial analysis, and data visualization to investigate where educational opportunities exist, where gaps remain, and how data can support more informed decision-making.


Research Questions

* How does access to educational opportunities vary across the state of North Carolina?
* What geographic, demographic, or socioeconomic factors are associated with differences in educational access?
* How do school choice options, including virtual education, affect educational opportunity?
* What role can data and spatial analysis play in supporting educational equity?
